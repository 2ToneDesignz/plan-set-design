01

Permit Processing

Keep your project moving through applications, plan checks, agency follow-ups, and revisions. We coordinate the permit process and bring the right professionals into the conversation.

Discuss this service
  • Pre-submission review of application requirements
  • Building, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, and grading application coordination
  • Liaison with planners, plan checkers, and inspectors
  • Tracking correction sheets and coordinating revised submissions
Primary deliverableOrganized permit package, submission tracking, and a clear correction log.
02

Blueprint Reading & Analysis

We review architectural, structural, and mechanical, electrical, and plumbing drawings to identify discrepancies, unclear details, and scope gaps before they become field issues.

Discuss this service
  • Cross-discipline drawing review
  • Constructability and feasibility review with qualified professionals
  • Requests for information and discrepancy logs
  • Trade plan walkthroughs and technical briefings
Primary deliverableAnnotated plans and a focused RFI report.
03

Quantity Takeoff & Estimating

Digital takeoffs turn schematic and construction drawings into organized material quantities, helping you compare bids and make informed scope decisions.

Discuss this service
  • Itemized lumber, concrete, steel, finishes, and utility quantities
  • Labor allowances with stated assumptions
  • Subcontractor bid comparisons and variance review
  • Material alternatives and value engineering coordination
Primary deliverableBill of quantities and an itemized estimate with documented assumptions.
04

3D Architectural Rendering

Interior and exterior visualizations help you compare materials, understand proportions, and make design decisions before construction.

Discuss this service
  • Interior living spaces, kitchens, and exterior views
  • Material, texture, lighting, and shadow studies
  • Site and landscape context
  • Panoramic views and walkthrough options by scope
Primary deliverableHigh-resolution visualization package suited to your project.
05

BIM Modeling & Coordination

Coordinated building models bring framing and building systems into one view, making potential clashes easier to identify and discuss with the design team.

Discuss this service
  • BIM development with an agreed level of detail
  • Framing, HVAC, and plumbing clash review
  • Construction sequencing studies where requested
  • As-built model coordination
Primary deliverableCoordinated model package and a prioritized clash log.
06

Client Advocacy & Construction Oversight

Independent client representation helps you stay informed as drawings become construction, with clear observations, scope reviews, and coordination through closeout.

Discuss this service
  • Subcontractor scope and proposal review
  • Job walks, progress logs, and milestone observations
  • Payment and change-order review support
  • Inspection liaison, correction tracking, and punch-list coordination
Primary deliverableSite observations, budget variance summaries, and milestone records.
